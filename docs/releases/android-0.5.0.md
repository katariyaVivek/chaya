Chaya 0.5.0 for Android: a new look, and everything Chaya for Windows does with what you saved.

- **A new look and icon.** Stage's colours and the two-suns icon; one header and one bar on every screen; Home opens
  on what you were watching, the Library on what you saved, as cards by day.
- **A player of its own.** A saved video plays in Chaya and starts again where you stopped. Held sideways it fills
  the screen. Subtitles saved with a video are drawn over it and listed under it as a transcript; tap a line to play
  from there. A video's chapters break the seek bar.
- **Subtitles made on your phone** for videos that have none, heard on the phone itself: Parakeet for English and 24
  other European languages, Whisper for the rest. They keep being made in the background, with Stop. Or, with your
  own Groq or OpenAI key, made by that service in seconds; Chaya says before anything is sent that the sound goes to
  it, and what it costs.
- **Your own AI model**, its key encrypted on your phone. With it, **translate** a video's subtitles and read the
  translation under the original, whole sentences at a time; and **find highlights**, marked on the seek bar.
- **Clips** from lines in the transcript or from a highlight, trimmed on a timeline, and **exported** as a video with
  its subtitles drawn on, or as SRT.
- **Subtitles put right** in the player: correct a line, take one out, or move a track's timing, with Undo. Delete
  subtitles, translations or speech models you no longer want.
- **Find what was said:** the Library's search finds words said in every saved video, and a line opens the video
  there. **Your own videos** from the phone join the Library too.
- **The browser:** a menu for links and pictures, *Search in new tab*, pull to reload, and Web search on chosen
  words on every phone.
- **Fixes:** a crash on Android 8 to 13 is gone, saving a link no longer closes the app, and pausing while picture
  and sound are joined is kept.

Nothing is uploaded unless you choose your own AI service, and there is no account: see the privacy policy.

**Install:** download app-release.apk on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from your
browser. An installed Chaya 0.4.0, or a preview, is updated in place and keeps your downloads.
