Chaya for Windows 0.3.0: a new look, and clips that go on to your editor.

- **Stage, a new look.** A sidebar holds Chaya's places: Home, Browse and the Library. Home opens on the video you
  were watching, with how far you got, and the newest saved beneath it. The Library keeps everything you saved, as
  cards by day, and opens as a tab of its own.
- **A new mark**, two suns, that shows how your downloads are going: its line draws across as they go, a stream
  sends a light across the sun, and it settles when they finish.
- **Found on this page** sits beside the page instead of over it: the video's picture and name, one choice of
  quality (1080p first) and one Save. The page keeps playing beside it.
- **The player and the clip editor**, redrawn: the controls sit under the picture, and choosing a clip turns the
  player into its editor, the clip lit in amber across a timeline the width of the window.
- **Editor projects.** *Export* › *Editor project* saves the clips you chose, in order, as one file for Final Cut Pro
  or DaVinci Resolve, or for Premiere Pro (its subtitles beside it, to import as captions). Nothing is re-encoded:
  the editor uses your video where it is, so keep the project with it.
- **Subtitles that follow the words.** For subtitles Chaya made, four new looks when you burn them into a clip:
  *Highlight* lights the word being said, *Karaoke* fills the words as they are said, *Pop* lights it a little
  larger, and *One at a time* shows a few words at a time, large.
- **Subtitles made on a laptop's own graphics card**, not the slower one built into its processor, and faster
  everywhere. Their progress says *Getting ready*, then how far it has got and about how long is left.
- **Smaller things:** drop-downs and scrollbars of Chaya's own, one panel open at a time, no black window flashing
  up when a clip is saved or the player opens, and smoother movement throughout.

Everything still happens on your PC unless you choose your own AI service. A copy of 0.2.0 offers this update in its
settings within a day.
