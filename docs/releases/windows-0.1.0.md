The first release of Chaya for Windows: the phone app's downloader, on the PC.

- **Links from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X and Pinterest** are looked up on your PC and offered in every
  quality they come in, picture and sound joined into one file, 4K included (as WebM where the site has only
  VP9). Posts save every picture and video; an Instagram or X account saves as one ZIP.
- **A browser** with tabs, that blocks ads (EasyList and EasyPrivacy) and finds the videos, sounds and streams
  on a page for you to save; video sites' pages are looked up as their links are, with your sign-in when you
  choose it.
- **Downloads** that pause, resume and retry, in sections by day, narrowed by kind, site and name, as a list or a
  grid of tiles with a picture viewer.
- **Updates from inside the app:** Chaya checks once a day and offers a new version in its settings.

Nothing is uploaded, and there is no account: see the privacy policy.
