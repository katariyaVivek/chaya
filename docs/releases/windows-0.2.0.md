Chaya for Windows 0.2.0: watch, understand and clip what you saved. Everything here happens on your PC unless you
choose your own AI service, and nothing is uploaded.

- **A player of its own.** A saved video plays in Chaya with its subtitles drawn over it and listed beside it; a line
  clicked plays from there, and a video starts again where you stopped. A video's chapters, from the site, break the
  seek bar where each begins; Ctrl+← and Ctrl+→ go between them.
- **Subtitles made on your PC** by whisper.cpp, in short lines that follow the speech, on your graphics card when it
  can (and on the processor when it can't), for one video or for every download. Pick the model in Settings; each is
  fetched once and can be deleted.
- **Your own AI model** (OpenAI, Anthropic, Gemini, DeepSeek, OpenRouter, Mistral, Groq, xAI, another service, or
  Ollama and LM Studio on your PC), its key kept in Windows Credential Manager. With it, **translate** a video's
  subtitles and watch the original and the translation together, each language in its own colour, font and size;
  and **find highlights**, the moments worth watching, marked on the seek bar.
- **Clips.** Choose lines in the transcript, or drag across the timeline under the picture (with its frames, its
  sound, its lines and its chapters), and trim between handles that snap to lines and chapters. A highlight or a
  chapter becomes a clip in one click.
- **Export** clips as videos with their subtitles drawn on (one language or two, in three looks, at 720p, 1080p or
  as they are), made to fit 10, 25 or 50 MB when you ask, with their loudness evened; as GIFs; or as SRT files, one
  per language or both in one. Videos use Windows' own H.264 encoder, on the graphics card first.
- **Subtitles put right:** correct a line's words, take one out, or move a whole track's timing, shifted or stretched
  between two lines you put right, with Undo.
- **Your own videos:** drop a video or sound from your PC on Chaya, or *Add from this PC*; it stays where it is and
  gets everything a download gets.
- **Find what was said:** the find box over your downloads searches every line of every video's subtitles, and a line
  found opens the video there.
- **Copied links:** turn on Ctrl+Alt+D in Settings, and the link you just copied, anywhere in Windows, is saved at the
  quality you chose, without Chaya coming to the front.
