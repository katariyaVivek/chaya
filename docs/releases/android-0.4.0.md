Chaya 0.4.0 for Android: links from video sites, a library of what you saved, tabs, and an ad blocker.

- **Links from YouTube, Instagram, TikTok, X and Pinterest.** Paste or share one, or open its page in the
  browser, and Chaya looks it up on your phone with yt-dlp, then offers every quality; picture and sound come
  out as one MP4. A post saves all its pictures and videos, and an Instagram or X account saves as one ZIP.
  Your sign-in is used only when you choose it. yt-dlp keeps itself current, about once a day.
- **A better media list.** The page's main video comes first with its real name; ads and a stream's pieces
  are folded away, and each quality is listed once. Players embedded from other servers are found too.
- **Streams saved as real MP4 files**, with their size estimated and a real progress bar. YouTube downloads
  come in pieces, three at a time, so they are not held to playback speed.
- **Downloads as a library:** a grid of what you saved, a picture viewer, an account's ZIP opened to its files,
  filters by kind and site, search, Share, and several chosen at once.
- **A browser with tabs** (they come back after Android closes the app), bookmarks, history kept on your phone
  only, and suggestions while you type.
- **An ad blocker** (EasyList and EasyPrivacy) that blocks ad and tracker requests, hides ad boxes and refuses
  ad pop-ups; on or off per site from the address bar's shield.
- **A new look and icon**, with Light, Dark or Same as phone.
- A page whose renderer crashes no longer closes the app.

Nothing is uploaded and there is no account: see the privacy policy.

**Install:** download app-release.apk on your phone and open it. Android asks once to allow installs from
your browser. An installed Chaya 0.3.0 is updated in place, and keeps your downloads.
