Color

Two grounds, one light

Day is warm paper under frosted white; Night is near-black under smoked glass. The accent is the same amber in both, used only as glow, edges and thin progress, never as a filled shape. On Day it is too pale for text or focus rings (1.9:1), so those use the text color there.

Type

Geist to read, Geist Mono to count

Three UI sizes and no more. Every number that changes (sizes, speeds, percentages, times) is in Geist Mono with tabular figures, so it does not jitter as it counts. Cabinet Grotesk 600 is kept for page titles and the wordmark.

Shape and space

Radii 10, 14, 20; everything on 8

Small radius for thumbnails and chips, medium for rows and fields, large for cards and windows. Pills are fully round. Spacing steps in eights; the 4px half step is only for an icon beside its label.

Motion

Things change shape, they do not jump

Enter 220 ms, exit 160 ms, hover 120 ms, all on one ease-out. Presses spring. Lists arrive 30 ms apart. With reduced motion, from Windows or from Chaya's own setting, only opacity changes.

Press

Scale 0.97 on a spring, stiffness 400 and damping 30. CSS traces the spring with linear(), so it settles in 453 ms with 2.8% overshoot, too little to read as bounce.

Lists

Each child fades in and rises 6px, 30 ms after the one before.

Components

Four moments to get right

Download button

Click to start. The pill shrinks into a ring counting in Geist Mono. Click the ring to pause: it stops and dims to half. At the end a check draws in, the button glows once, then settles back to a pill.

State: idle

Finding media

Finding media…

A warm light sweeps across the words every 1.6 s while a page or link is looked up. With reduced motion the words breathe instead.

Address and search

Focus the field: amber light fades in underneath, softened by the glass. Its edge turns to the focus color so focus never depends on the glow.

Download row

While a download runs, a thin light circles its border once every 8 s. Paused, the light stays where it stopped, at half strength. Everything else has the plain hairline.
